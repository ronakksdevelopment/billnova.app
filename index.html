      User Guide
      Scanning products

      Open the Scan tab. The camera starts right away, so just point it at a QR code or barcode. A known product is added to the bill instantly. A new code asks for a name and price once, then every future scan of that code adds it automatically.

      Adding items manually

      Tap the plus button near the bottom of the Scan screen to add a product without a code. Enter a name, price and quantity, then add it to the bill.

      Building the bill

      Adjust quantity with the plus and minus buttons on each item, add a discount or GST percentage if needed, and check the grand total in the summary bar before confirming.

      Taking UPI payment

      Add your UPI ID in Shop Details above. When confirming a bill, choose UPI as the payment method and a QR code with the exact amount appears for the customer to scan and pay with any UPI app.

      Printing a receipt

      After a bill is completed, tap Print Receipt on the success screen to open the print dialog and print directly to a connected printer.

      Sharing a receipt

      Tap Share on WhatsApp on the success screen to send the receipt image straight to a customer's chat.

      Saving a receipt

      Tap Save as PDF on the success screen to download a clean copy of the receipt for your records.
